Two years have passed since Queen Aria fell into her coma. Elena is now seventeen and has become remarkably skilled with fire magic. She can summon flames powerful enough to melt steel, and her control has improved tremendously under Eldrin's guidance.

Marcus completed his masterwork: a sword that can cut through magical barriers. He presented it to Elena, saying it might help her defend the kingdom when she comes of age. Their friendship has grown even stronger over the years.

Last week, disturbing news arrived from the northern territories. Malkor, the dark sorcerer who wounded Queen Aria, has returned with an army of shadow creatures. The villages are calling for help, and the royal council is divided on how to respond.

Lord Darius wants to negotiate with Malkor, believing that diplomacy might spare lives. However, Elena feels differently. She remembers her mother's sacrifice and wants to confront the sorcerer directly. Eldrin has counseled patience, reminding her of the lessons about emotional control.

Yesterday, Elena made a decision. Despite being only seventeen and not yet of age to rule, she gathered a small group of loyal guards and prepared to ride north. Marcus insisted on accompanying her, bringing the magical sword he crafted.

Before they departed, Elena visited her mother's bedside. Queen Aria remained unconscious, her breathing steady but shallow. Elena held her hand and whispered, "I will make Malkor pay for what he did to you. I will burn him with flames so hot that nothing will remain."

As they rode toward the northern territories, Elena's fire magic seemed stronger than ever before, fueled by her anger and desire for revenge. Marcus noticed her eyes glowing with an intense orange light he had never seen before. "Elena, remember what Eldrin taught us," he said cautiously.

"I remember everything," Elena replied. "Sometimes fire must destroy before it can forge new beginnings. That's what mother said."

The journey would take three days. Elena spent her evenings practicing increasingly destructive fire spells, pushing her magic to its absolute limits.
